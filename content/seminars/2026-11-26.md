title: Scythe: Semantic-Aware Rewriting for Fast, Effective Program Reduction
presenter:  Ioanna Moraiti
date: 2026-11-26
time: 18:00
category: seminars

Automated program reduction shrinks a program into a smaller one that preserves a property of interest. Language-agnostic grammar-based reducers are easy to retarget across languages, but because they reason only about syntax, they cannot perform the coupled edits needed to remove whole declarations, e.g., a method cannot be deleted while it is still called. As a result, they waste oracle calls on edits that cannot succeed and often leave irrelevant declarations behind. We present Scythe, a fast, semantic-aware pre-processing step that runs in front of any grammar-based reducer without modifying it. Scythe encodes the program as a typed declaration dependency graph and performs a set of semantic-aware operators that decouple declarations from their use sites, making them removable while keeping the program well-typed. Fine-grained token-level minimization is then left to the downstream reducer. On 73 benchmarks across C, Java, and Solidity, combining Scythe with Perses never yields a larger result than Perses alone, and a strictly smaller one on 70 of 73 cases. On average, the combination produces programs 55.6% smaller (up to 95.5%) and is 2.3× faster (up to 7.4×). Scythe also boosts Latra, a reducer that, unlike the purely syntactic Perses, also applies language-specific transformations: the combination produces programs 10.6% smaller (up to 61.8%) and is 2.4× faster on average.
