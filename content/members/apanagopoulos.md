**Alexandros Panagopoulos** is a PhD candidate at the Athens University of Economics and Business. He holds a BSc in Statistics and Insurance Science from the University of Piraeus and an MSc in Finance and Banking from Athens University of Economics and Business. His research interests include financial econometrics, financial market efficiency, machine learning and high performance computing.
